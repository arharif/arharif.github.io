## Assessment and implementation

The X1 implementation rating runs from 0 to 4. It averages rated, applicable entries and keeps evidence status, assessed coverage, risk and legal conclusions distinct. Risk prioritisation uses separately entered likelihood and impact. A work package considers linked active residual risk and assessed gaps. Approval and validation milestones depend on the linked scope and remediation work. Estimates use one workday per gap point, subject to scheduler limits, with editable duration, effort, owner, dependencies and evidence. ISO Annex A references do not substitute for the organisation's assessment and selection of necessary controls.

Sample and personal projects use separate browser storage slots. Sample exports identify their contents, and replacing a saved personal assessment asks for confirmation. Academic projects remain browser-local. The patch does not modify Supabase permissions, authentication, publishing or storage code.
