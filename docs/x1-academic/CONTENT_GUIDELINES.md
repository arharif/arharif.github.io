# Content authoring and review

Add published modules to `data/curriculum.ts` only after completing prerequisites, measurable objectives, analogy, substantive explanations, practical activity, knowledge question with explanatory feedback, misconceptions, revision sheet, laboratory and authoritative source. Add planned titles to their discipline without a module record. The interface labels these as planned. A module's stable ID is also used in progress records and links.

Current published modules are `incident-triage`, `risk-assessment` and `detection-evaluation`. Each has four teaching sections and an activity deliverable. Review technical meaning, business assumptions and answer explanations before changing their publication status. More assessment formats, richer diagrams and deeper lesson sequences remain on the roadmap.

Use the case registry for names, services, owners, suppliers and constraints. Keep the same identity and operational boundaries across labs. Every synthetic record and export carries the fictional-scenario disclaimer. Avoid personal, employer, client or operational security records.

Resources have a stable ID, title, description, domain, topic, type, difficulty, access, publisher, original URL, duration status, metadata review date, verification status, framework, role, skills and related lesson. Unknown duration is explicitly self-paced. Additions should teach a concrete objective and originate from a traceable publisher.

Run `npm run check:links`. The report records check time, response status or connection error for each seed link. A successful HTTP response establishes reachability only. Review the document's edition, applicability, access conditions and educational relevance separately. Current checks were blocked by the environment egress proxy. Keep verification pending until a real review succeeds. Never change a review timestamp to imply an unperformed edition check.

Do not reproduce licensed ISO standards. Use original paraphrases, identify the publication, and direct readers to the publisher. Mappings must declare their source, confidence and interpretation. Frameworks and standards serve different purposes. A selected teaching subset cannot support a general conformity conclusion.

Certification records retain existing preparation guidance. Four records link to issuer programmes. Programme names, eligibility, current exams and prices need publisher review before being represented as verified facts. Never promise employment or substitute an academy activity for an issuer requirement.
